Introducing Mureka 9.5

August 10, 2026
New featureMusic modelWebiOSAndroid

Mureka 9.5 is our latest music generation model, delivering more polished results and more reliable creative control from the first prompt to the final song.

  • More natural vocals: Clearer phrasing, smoother transitions, and more expressive performances.
  • Better prompt understanding: More accurate responses to genre, mood, instrumentation, and production instructions.

  • Improved audio quality: Cleaner, more balanced generations across a wider range of musical styles.
